Questions

Common Doubts.

Any student enrolled in JNGEC Sundernagar (1st or 2nd year) can voluntarily join NSS. There is no selection test — only sincere commitment to community service is required.

You need to complete 240 hours of community service over 2 years (120 hours/year) plus one mandatory 7-day residential special camp to earn your NSS certificate.

NSS activities are scheduled on weekends and holidays. The program is designed to complement, not conflict with, your academic schedule. Most volunteers report improved time management skills.

Upon completion, you receive an official NSS Certificate recognized by the Government of India. This gives bonus points in UPSC, university admissions, government jobs, and CSR hiring.
